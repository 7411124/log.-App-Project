How it works

Three steps.
That's it.

01

Sign up with your NHS email

Use your institutional or NHS email. We verify you're a registered surgical trainee or consultant before activating your account.

02

Log your first case

Tap +, fill in the procedure name, your role, and supervision level. Add optional fields like approach, urgency, and operative findings.

03

Track progress & export

Watch your stats grow. When it's portfolio time, export everything as a CSV in one tap.
